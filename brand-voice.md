# Brand Voice — Gymshark (DEMO / FICTIONAL DATA)

> ⚠️ This is sample data for a product demo. Numbers and rankings are illustrative, not real Gymshark metrics.

## Identity
- **Name:** Gymshark
- **Domain:** gymshark.com
- **Niche:** Fitness apparel and accessories — direct-to-consumer gym wear for lifters, runners, and everyday athletes

## Positioning — What We're Known For
- **Conditioning-tested fit** — squat-proof, sweat-wicking, seamless construction designed and tested by real athletes
- **Community-built brand** — grown through athletes and lifters, not billboards
- Our unfair advantage: we design *with* a global community of athletes, so the fit and feel are validated in real training before they ship — not designed in a boardroom.

## Core Topics
- Gym leggings, squat-proof leggings, seamless leggings, scrunch leggings
- Men's gym wear — gym shorts, oversized gym t-shirts, training hoodies
- Women's gym wear, workout clothes, activewear
- Sustainable activewear, fabric technology, fit guides

## Voice & Tone
- Motivating, confident, and inclusive — we speak to people mid-journey, not just elite athletes
- Direct and practical — we explain fit, fabric, and use-case, not hype
- We sound like a training partner who's honest about what works
- Prefer "here's how it performs in a real session" over generic product copy

## Customer Experience
- **Audience:** lifters, runners, and everyday gym-goers (men and women, 18–34 core) who train regularly and care about fit, performance, and how kit looks
- **Pain points:** leggings that go see-through on squats, seams that chafe, sizing that's inconsistent, gym wear that doesn't survive heavy training
- **What they value:** squat-proof confidence, true-to-size fit, durability, and a look they're proud to train in
- What we know from doing the work: the #1 reason a customer trusts a legging is the squat test — if it passes that, everything else (color, price, brand) becomes secondary.

## Customer Language
- "squat proof", "true to size", "seamless", "scrunch", "sweat wicking", "high waisted", "buttery soft", "gym fit", "lifting", "PR"

## Things We Never Say
- No body-shaming or "earn your body" messaging — training is for everyone
- We never over-promise on fabric claims we haven't tested
- No generic filler with no point of view on fit or performance
